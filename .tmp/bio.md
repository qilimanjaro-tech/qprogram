## Extensive

I am crafting quantum software for superconducting systems.

At Qilimanjaro Quantum Tech, I contribute to the design and development of core quantum software infrastructure. A lot of that work is about designing novel abstractions and paradigms that make quantum hardware programmable across the full stack — from pulse-level quantum control of superconducting qubits, through the SDKs that researchers actually want to use, up to integration with heterogeneous compute and HPC environments needed to run experiments at scale. In practice, this also means thinking carefully about API design, developer experience, packaging and distribution, and the runtime environments needed to execute workloads reliably.

I am also doing my PhD research on pulse optimization and gate design for high-fidelity quantum control in superconducting qubits. This is a natural continuation of the work I have already been doing. My research focuses on how to design better control strategies that improve performance on real hardware while remaining robust, physically meaningful, and scalable across platforms.

Alongside my engineering and research work, I have a strong background in training, technical consulting, and public speaking. I have delivered talks and technical sessions for different audiences and I enjoy explaining difficult ideas in a way that is clear and useful. I have also mentored teams in hackathons, incubators, and innovation programs, helping people shape ideas, make technical decisions, and turn concepts into working solutions. I serve on the Advisory Board of DevNetwork.
