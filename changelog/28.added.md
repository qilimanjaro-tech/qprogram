Eight worked example pages: qubit spectroscopy, T1 and Ramsey, active reset, resonator spectroscopy, CPMG on two qubits, multiplexed readout, single-shot readout, and checking a program before it runs. Between them they give a complete program to the parts of the language the earlier examples never reached: `set_frequency` and `qp.Linspace`, arithmetic expressions over a swept variable, `wait` as a time axis, `reset_phase` and `set_phase`, `MeasurementField.STATE` read back as a population and at single-shot resolution, the `if_` / `elif_` / `else_` chain conditioned on a measurement's classified state, `set_parameter` with the `forced-host` warning and the `qp.optimize` rewrite that clears it, `@qp.fragment` with `expand()`, `qp.WaveformLibrary` and the `.wfl` format, `QProgram.rebind`, a hand-written `qp.MeasurementModel`, and a `qp.PlatformCapabilities` built by hand to show what `qp.validate` refuses.
